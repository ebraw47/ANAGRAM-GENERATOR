# Title Capitalization Rules: APA, Chicago, AP & MLA Compared

Title case is not one universal standard. Style manuals — AMA, AP, APA, Bluebook, Chicago, MLA, NY Times, and Wikipedia — each define which words count as major or minor. This guide summarizes the shared rules and the differences that matter when you publish.

![Title capitalization rules comparison](/title-case-converter-capitalization.webp)

## Shared title case baseline

Most guides agree on this core:

1. Always capitalize the **first word** of a title or subtitle  
2. Capitalize **nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs**  
3. Lowercase **articles** (*a*, *an*, *the*) mid-title  
4. Capitalize short verbs and pronouns (*Is*, *Are*, *It*, *My*) even when they look “small”

Everything else — especially prepositions and conjunctions — is where guides diverge.

## Style comparison table

| Rule | AMA / APA / AP | Chicago / Bluebook / Wikipedia | MLA | NY Times |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short prepositions (≤3 letters) | Lowercase | Lowercase | Lowercase | Selective list |
| 4-letter prepositions (*from*, *with*) | Often capitalize | Usually lowercase | Lowercase | Usually capitalize |
| Long prepositions | Capitalize | Style-dependent | Lowercase all | Capitalize |
| FANBOYS | Lowercase all seven | Chicago capitalizes *yet*, *so* | Lowercase all | Mix (*nor/yet/so* up) |
| Last word always capped | No (AMA/APA/Bluebook) | Yes (Chicago/Wiki) | Yes | Yes |

## Prepositions

- **AMA, APA, AP:** lowercase prepositions of three letters or fewer (*in*, *on*, *off*, *out*). Longer ones (*With*, *From*, *About*) are usually capitalized.  
- **Chicago, Bluebook, Wikipedia:** also lowercase many four-letter prepositions.  
- **MLA:** lowercase **all** prepositions, regardless of length.  
- **NY Times:** short selective list (*at*, *by*, *in*, *for*…) stays lowercase; *up*, *off*, *out* and longer prepositions are capitalized.

## Coordinating and subordinating conjunctions

FANBOYS (*for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so*) are lowercase in AMA, AP, APA, MLA, Bluebook, and Wikipedia. **Chicago** capitalizes *yet* and *so*. **NY Times** capitalizes *nor*, *yet*, and *so*.

For *as* and *if*: AP, APA, and NY Times often lowercase both; Chicago lowercases *as* but capitalizes *if*; AMA, Bluebook, MLA, and Wikipedia typically capitalize both.

## Last word and hyphenated compounds

AP, Chicago, MLA, NY Times, and Wikipedia capitalize the final word even if it is minor. AMA, APA, and Bluebook do not — so a trailing preposition can stay lowercase (*Wish for*).

Hyphenated compounds also vary (*Self-Report* vs *Self-report*). When your publisher specifies a guide, convert with that exact style.
