# Fertile Window Explained: When Are You Most Fertile?

The **fertile window** is the stretch of days when unprotected sex is most likely to lead to pregnancy. Authoritative guides (ACOG, Johns Hopkins, Office on Women’s Health) describe about **six key days** per cycle.

## Why about six days?

- Sperm can live up to about **five days**.  
- An egg usually lives **12–24 hours** after ovulation.  
- Together that covers the days leading up to ovulation and ovulation day itself.

## When odds peak

Daily fecundability research and clinical summaries often place the highest chances in the **one to two days before** ovulation — and still strong on ovulation day.

## How to find your window

1. Run the [ovulation calculator](/).  
2. Note the highlighted fertile days.  
3. Confirm with [cervical mucus](/cervical-mucus-and-fertility) or [OPKs](/how-to-use-an-ovulation-test) when possible.

## Timing tip

Have sex every day or every other day across the window rather than waiting for a single “perfect” date. Read [best time to conceive](/best-time-to-conceive).
