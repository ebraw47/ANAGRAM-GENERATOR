# Wordle Strategy: Tips and Tricks That Shrink the List Faster

Winning Wordle more often is less about knowing obscure words and more about **information gain**. These tips mirror what top-ranking strategy pages teach — fixed openers, scout seconds, duplicate awareness, and smart use of a [Wordle word finder](/).

## Tip 1 — Use one fixed starting word

Rotating openers every day resets your intuition. Choose a strong five-unique-letter word from our [starting words guide](/best-wordle-starting-words) and stick with it for a month.

## Tip 2 — Make guess two a scout

Unless you already have three or more greens, guess two should usually test **new letters**, not just rearrange early yellows into a pretty near-miss. Full playbook: [second-guess strategy](/wordle-second-guess-strategy).

## Tip 3 — Never reuse confirmed gray letters

Gray means that copy is done. Replaying eliminated letters wastes a slot you could spend testing something unknown.

## Tip 4 — Read yellows as position bans

A yellow tile is not “free floating.” It forbids the square it appeared on. Entering that ban into a finder is often the difference between 30 candidates and 4.

## Tip 5 — Respect duplicate-letter traps

If a letter shows colored once and gray once in the same guess, the answer may contain **exactly one** copy. Details: [repeated letters explained](/wordle-repeated-letters-explained).

## Tip 6 — Use frequency when the list is long

When many words remain, target letters that appear most often across the remaining set. Our finder surfaces those “hot letters” automatically.

## Tip 7 — Save rare letters for late

Q, J, X, Z (and often V) appear in a tiny share of answers. Burning an early guess on them rarely pays unless clues already force them.

## Tip 8 — Pattern traps need splitter guesses

Families like `_IGHT` or `_ATCH` can hide many answers that differ by one letter. In normal mode, spend a guess that tests several possible first letters at once. In hard mode, you may have to grind them one by one.

## Tip 9 — Know when to open a finder

Use a helper when:

- You have conflicting yellow constraints in your head
- The board looks “solved” but several lookalikes remain
- You are on guess 5 and cannot afford a mental blank

Skip it when you are learning basic color rules — struggle is useful until the rules click. See [how Wordle colors work](/how-wordle-colors-work).

## Tip 10 — Hard mode is a different game

Hard mode forbids pure scouts. Strategy shifts toward committing earlier and reading the candidate list more carefully ([hard mode guide](/wordle-hard-mode-guide)).

## A simple daily routine

1. Play your fixed opener  
2. Enter clues into the [Wordle word finder](/) if needed  
3. Scout or commit based on remaining count  
4. Finish without reusing grays or ignoring yellow bans  

Master these habits and your average score improves even if your vocabulary stays the same.
