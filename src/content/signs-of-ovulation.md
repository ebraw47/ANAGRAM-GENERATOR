# Signs of Ovulation: Body Clues You Can Track

Calendar tools estimate timing. Your body can add real-time clues — a theme covered by WebMD, Cleveland Clinic, and Tommy’s ovulation pages.

## More reliable clues

1. **Cervical mucus** — clearer, stretchy, egg-white texture near peak fertility.  
2. **LH surge** — positive [ovulation test](/how-to-use-an-ovulation-test), often 24–36 hours before ovulation.  
3. **Basal body temperature rise** — small increase after ovulation that helps confirm it happened ([BBT guide](/basal-body-temperature-tracking)).

## Other possible signs

- Mild one-sided pelvic twinges  
- Breast tenderness  
- Bloating  
- Light spotting  
- Temporary rise in sex drive  

Tommy’s notes that pain, spotting, breast changes, and mood shifts are **less reliable alone** and can have other causes.
