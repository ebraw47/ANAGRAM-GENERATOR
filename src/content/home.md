## What Is an Anagram?

An **anagram** is a word or phrase made by rearranging all the letters of another word or phrase, using every letter **exactly once**. The word comes from the Greek *ana* (“again”) and *gramma* (“letter”) — literally a re-lettering.

Classic single-word pairs:

| Letters | Anagrams |
| --- | --- |
| listen | silent, enlist, tinsel |
| earth | heart, hater |
| night | thing |
| elbow | below, bowel |
| stressed | desserts |

Phrase anagrams use the same rule across several words:

| Subject | Phrase anagram |
| --- | --- |
| dormitory | dirty room |
| the eyes | they see |
| astronomer | moon starer |
| schoolmaster | the classroom |
| eleven plus two | twelve plus one |

People reach for anagrams to win word games, crack crossword and Jumble clues, decode hidden messages, and play with names. A famous example rearranges **Clint Eastwood** into **Old West Action**.

## How to Solve Anagrams by Hand (Then Verify)

Before you open the tool — or after you want to train your eye — use the habits solvers share across SERP guides:

1. **Rewrite the letters** in a circle or alphabetical order to break the original pattern  
2. **Pull rare letters first** (Q, J, X, Z) and build around them  
3. **Spot prefixes and suffixes** (-ING, -ED, -ER, -TION, RE-, UN-)  
4. **Group digraphs** like TH, CH, SH, NG, QU  
5. **Separate vowels and consonants**, then slot vowels into consonant frames  

When you are stuck, generate the list above and study what you missed. Practice tips: [how to solve anagrams](/how-to-solve-anagrams).
