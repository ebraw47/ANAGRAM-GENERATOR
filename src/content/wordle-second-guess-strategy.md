# Best Wordle Second Guess Strategy: Scout Before You Commit

Your opener gets the headlines. Your **second guess** decides whether the board collapses or sprawls. Ranking strategy articles agree on a counterintuitive rule: after guess one, do not automatically “build” the prettiest word from your early greens and yellows — **scout** first.

## What a scout guess is

A scout (sometimes called a probe or information guess) is a word chosen to test **new high-value letters**, even if it temporarily ignores a green you already found (normal mode only).

Goal of guess two: maximize new information so guess three can commit.

## When to scout

Scout when:

- You have 0–2 greens  
- Many letters remain untested  
- The obvious “almost answer” still leaves a huge family of lookalikes  

Commit earlier when:

- You already have 3+ greens  
- Hard mode forbids scouts ([hard mode guide](/wordle-hard-mode-guide))  
- The [Wordle word finder](/) shows fewer than ~10 common candidates  

## How to build a strong second guess

1. List letters you have **not** tested.  
2. Prefer hot consonants/vowels still common in English answers.  
3. Avoid reusing gray letters.  
4. In normal mode, it is often fine to temporarily not replay every yellow if your scout tests five fresh tiles — then reconcile on guess three.  
5. In hard mode, every yellow/green must appear; pick the remaining candidate that still splits the set best.

## Pair scouting with a finder

After guess one:

1. Enter clues into the finder.  
2. Read the **hot letters** strip.  
3. Build a scout that hits as many hot unknowns as possible.  
4. After guess two feedback, update the finder and commit.

This is the practical loop top helper sites describe as information gain.

## Example pattern (illustrative)

Opener `SLATE` returns two yellows and no greens. Instead of forcing both yellows into a pretty word that reuses weak consonants, play a second word loaded with untested frequent letters (for example a CRANE/ROUND-style probe depending on what remains). Then let the finder finish the job.

## Mistakes to avoid on guess two

- Confirming a green you already trust  
- Retesting the same yellow square  
- Panicking into rare letters (J/Q/X/Z) too early  
- Ignoring that duplicates may still be possible  

## Bottom line

Guess one opens the map. Guess two should explore the fog. Guess three should take the city. Use the live finder on [wordlewordfinder.net](/) whenever the fog is still thick.
