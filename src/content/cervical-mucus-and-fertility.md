# Cervical Mucus and Fertility: Reading Peak Fertility Clues

ACOG and fertility educators often call cervical mucus one of the most useful self-detection methods. Near ovulation, mucus may become clearer, thinner, and stretchy — like raw egg whites.

## Why it matters

Fertile-quality mucus helps sperm survive and travel. Noticing that shift can mark approaching peak fertility even when a calendar estimate is slightly off.

## Simple observation

- Check appearance and stretch once or twice daily.  
- Note dry, sticky, creamy, and egg-white patterns.  
- Mark fertile-type days beside your [calculator](/) results.

## Pair with other tools

Mucus + [OPKs](/how-to-use-an-ovulation-test) + calendar coverage is a practical trio for trying to conceive. Ask a clinician if patterns are hard to interpret or cycles are irregular.

Educational tracking is not a substitute for medical care or reliable contraception counseling.
