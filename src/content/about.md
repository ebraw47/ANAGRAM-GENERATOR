# About Anagram Generator

**Anagram Generator** on [anagramgenerator.tech](https://anagramgenerator.tech/) is a free, browser-based anagram solver and maker for players, puzzlers, teachers, writers, and anyone who loves letter play.

We built the tool to cover what top-ranking pages emphasize: **exact anagrams**, **subset unscrambling**, **multi-word phrases**, blank-tile wildcards, Scrabble scores, and clear guides — plus **six languages** and **voice playback** — without accounts or uploads.

## What we offer

- Exact, all-words, and multi-word anagram modes  
- Dictionaries and workspace UI in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Portuguese  
- Speak button on every result so you can hear pronunciation in the selected language  
- Minimum length filters, Scrabble-style scoring, and one-click copy  
- Private, in-browser search — letters stay on your device  
- Guides on definitions, famous examples, solve tips, and name anagrams  

## Our mission

Help people rearrange letters into real words and phrases quickly — for games, classrooms, creative writing, and curiosity — while keeping their input private and usable across major languages.
