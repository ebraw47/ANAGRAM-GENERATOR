# Ovulation Calendar: Track Your Most Fertile Days

An **ovulation calendar** maps fertile days onto your menstrual cycle. Medical publishers ranking for “ovulation calendar” (including Cleveland Clinic) describe it as a practical way to reduce guesswork when you are trying to conceive.

## Calendar vs calculator

They are closely related:

- A **calendar** emphasizes the month view of fertile and ovulation days.  
- A **calculator** emphasizes the inputs and the resulting dates.

Our [Cycle Studio](/) shows both: a dated estimate and a cycle map.

## How to build your calendar

1. Record day 1 of bleeding for several months.  
2. Average your cycle length.  
3. Estimate ovulation ~14 days before the next period.  
4. Mark the five days before ovulation plus ovulation day as your fertile stretch.

Example for a 28-day cycle: ovulation around day 14; fertile opportunities often days 9–14.

## When calendars struggle

Irregular periods, skipped cycles, or recent hormonal birth control can blur calendar timing. Add mucus tracking, OPKs, or clinical advice. See [irregular periods and ovulation](/irregular-periods-and-ovulation).

## Safety note

Do not use a calendar alone to prevent pregnancy.
