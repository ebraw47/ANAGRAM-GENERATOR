# Famous Anagram Examples: Words, Phrases & Names

![Famous anagram examples](/blog/famous-anagram-examples.webp)

The best anagrams are memorable because the new arrangement **relates** to the original — a joke, a synonym, a reveal, or a punchline. Below are the classics people search for, plus how to recreate them with an [anagram generator](/).

## Classic phrase anagrams

These are the examples ranking “anagram examples” pages almost always include:

- **dormitory** → dirty room  
- **astronomer** → moon starer  
- **schoolmaster** → the classroom  
- **a gentleman** → elegant man  
- **eleven plus two** → twelve plus one  
- **the Morse code** → here come dots  
- **conversation** → voices rant on  
- **a decimal point** → I’m a dot in place

## Literary and pop-culture anagrams

Writers hide anagrams as clues or Easter eggs. Dan Brown’s *The Da Vinci Code* uses letter rearrangements as plot devices; Edward Gorey published under anagrammatic pen names; Vladimir Nabokov slipped *Vivian Darkbloom* (an anagram of his own name) into *Lolita*.

## Antigrams (opposite meanings)

An **antigram** is an anagram whose meaning opposes the subject:

- restful → fluster  
- funeral → real fun  
- forty five → over fifty
