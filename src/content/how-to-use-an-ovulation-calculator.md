# How to Use an Ovulation Calculator: Step-by-Step Guide

An **ovulation calculator** helps you estimate when you may ovulate and which days are most fertile. Most calculators use the same two inputs: last period start and average cycle length.

## What you need

1. The **first day** of your last menstrual period (day 1 of bleeding).  
2. Your **average cycle length** — from day 1 of one period to day 1 of the next.

Track two to three cycles if you are unsure of your average.

## Steps on OvulationCalculator.io

1. Open the [Cycle Studio calculator](/).  
2. Tap the first day of your last period.  
3. Adjust the cycle-length slider if you are not on a 28-day cycle.  
4. Tap **Calculate fertile window**.  
5. Review ovulation day, fertile days, and next expected period.

## How the estimate is made

Most tools assume a luteal phase of about **14 days**. Ovulation ≈ cycle length − 14. Your fertile window is typically the **five days before ovulation plus ovulation day**.

## Make results more useful

- Recalculate after each new period.  
- Confirm with [signs of ovulation](/signs-of-ovulation) or an [ovulation test](/how-to-use-an-ovulation-test).  
- If cycles vary widely, read [irregular periods and ovulation](/irregular-periods-and-ovulation).

## Limits

Estimates are not medical advice and are **not birth control**. Talk with a clinician about pregnancy planning or contraception.
