# How to Use an Ovulation Test Kit (OPK) Correctly

**Ovulation predictor kits** detect a rise in luteinizing hormone (LH) before ovulation. This topic ranks strongly alongside calculator queries on WebMD, Cleveland Clinic, FDA, and clinic sites.

## How OPKs fit with a calculator

1. Estimate timing with the [ovulation calculator](/).  
2. Start testing a few days before the predicted fertile window (often ~3–5 days before expected ovulation).  
3. A positive surge usually means ovulation may follow within about **24–36 hours**.  
4. Plan intercourse that day and the next.

## Basic steps

1. Read your kit’s instructions — brands differ.  
2. Test at a consistent time; avoid over-hydrating beforehand.  
3. Dip the strip or midstream stick as directed.  
4. Read within the stated time window.  
5. Treat a test line as dark as/darker than the control (or your kit’s positive symbol) as a surge.

## Tips

- Know your cycle length so you do not waste strips too early.  
- Irregular cycles may need more tests.  
- Pair with [mucus tracking](/cervical-mucus-and-fertility).  
- If you never see a surge, ask a clinician.

OPKs predict a surge — they do not guarantee egg release every cycle. Not for contraception.
