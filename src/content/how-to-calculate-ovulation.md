# How to Calculate Ovulation From Your Cycle Length

You can estimate ovulation with simple cycle math used across ACOG, Cleveland Clinic, and Tommy’s guidance.

## The core rule

**Ovulation ≈ cycle length − 14** (luteal phase ≈ 14 days).

Day 1 = first day of bleeding.

## Examples

| Cycle length | Estimated ovulation |
| --- | --- |
| 21 days | Around day 7 |
| 26 days | Around day 12 |
| 28 days | Around day 14 |
| 32 days | Around day 18 |
| 35 days | Around day 21 |

## Fertile days from that estimate

Mark roughly five days before ovulation through ovulation day. For a 28-day cycle that is often days **9–14**.

## Average first

Track three cycles, average the lengths, then calculate. Or skip the arithmetic and use our [ovulation calculator](/).

## Caveats

Illness, stress, travel, and irregular cycles can move ovulation. Confirm with body signs or tests when timing matters.
