# About Title Case Converter

**Title Case Converter** on [titlecaseconverter.tech](https://titlecaseconverter.tech/) is a free, browser-based title capitalization tool for writers, students, marketers, editors, and publishers.

We built the converter to close the gap top-ranking tools cover well: **multiple style guides**, context-aware capitalization for tricky short words, multi-line batches, explanations, and SEO-friendly character counts — without accounts or uploads.

## What we offer

- Title case for AMA, AP, APA, Bluebook, Chicago, MLA, NY Times, and Wikipedia  
- Sentence case, UPPERCASE, and lowercase  
- Private, in-browser conversion  
- Guides on rules, words to capitalize, and title vs sentence case  

## Our mission

Help people publish clean, consistent titles — books, blogs, papers, product pages, and SEO meta titles — without memorizing every style-manual exception.
