# Basal Body Temperature Tracking for Ovulation

**Basal body temperature (BBT)** is your temperature at complete rest, usually taken right after waking. After ovulation, progesterone can nudge BBT upward by roughly **0.5–1°F** (about 0.2°C).

## What BBT tells you

A sustained rise often confirms that ovulation **already happened**. That makes BBT great for learning patterns — but less useful for predicting the exact day in advance.

## How to chart

1. Use a sensitive thermometer.  
2. Measure at the same time each morning before getting up.  
3. Log readings and look for a biphasic pattern.  
4. Compare confirmed days with [calculator estimates](/).

## Combine methods

Use the calculator and [LH tests](/how-to-use-an-ovulation-test) for forward-looking timing; use BBT to verify afterward. Illness, alcohol, poor sleep, and inconsistent timing can muddy charts — note disruptions when they happen.
