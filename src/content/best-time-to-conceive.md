# Best Time to Conceive: When to Have Sex Around Ovulation

If you are trying to conceive, timing sex around ovulation improves odds. ACOG and fertility organizations emphasize a multi-day window — not a single hour.

## Practical timing

- Highest odds often **1–2 days before** ovulation.  
- Sex in the days **leading up** still counts because sperm can wait.  
- Continue through estimated ovulation day.

Many clinicians suggest sex **every day or every other day** across the fertile window.

## How to pick those days

1. Use the [ovulation calculator](/).  
2. Review the fertile window.  
3. Watch for fertile-type [cervical mucus](/cervical-mucus-and-fertility).  
4. Optional: confirm with an [OPK](/how-to-use-an-ovulation-test).

## Lifestyle supports (not guarantees)

Healthy weight range when possible, managed medical conditions, moderate exercise, limited smoking/alcohol, and partner wellness all matter. Calculators help with timing — they cannot force conception.
